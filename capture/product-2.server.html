Key Active Ingredients:

  • Vitamin B3 (Niacinamide): Known for its skin-brightening and anti-inflammatory properties, Vitamin B3 helps to even out skin tone, reduce redness, and improve overall skin texture. It also strengthens the skin’s natural barrier, ensuring it remains hydrated and resilient.
  • Glycolic Acid: As a leading Alpha Hydroxy Acid (AHA), Glycolic Acid gently exfoliates the skin by dissolving the bonds between dead skin cells. This process promotes cell turnover, leaving your skin looking renewed and vibrant while minimizing the appearance of fine lines and blemishes.
  • Lactic Acid: This multifunctional ingredient not only acts as a gentle exfoliant but also works as a moisturizer and pH balancer. Lactic Acid helps to smooth and soften the skin, enhance its natural radiance, and maintain optimal hydration levels.
