Who We Are

Committed to enhancing your natural beauty.

Illumea Skincare is a premium cosmetic brand by Sansu International Pvt. Ltd., headquartered at 17/1/1 Primrose Road, Kandy, Sri Lanka. Our products are manufactured locally in Sri Lanka by a GMP-certified company, ensuring the highest standards of quality and safety.

At Illumea, we are committed to crafting skincare solutions that are 100% herbal, safe, and free from harmful chemicals or bleaching agents. Every ingredient in our formulations is carefully selected to provide effective and gentle care, aligning with our dedication to customer health and well-being.

After a successful launch in Italy in May 2024, Illumea is now set to grace the Sri Lankan market starting January 2025. Our mission is to deliver top-quality skincare products at affordable prices, making premium beauty accessible to everyone. At Illumea, your safety and satisfaction are our top priorities, as we strive to create a skincare experience that enhances your natural beauty with confidence.

From the Desk of Our Managing Director

Guiding Illumea with passion and a commitment to excellence.

Dr. Chaminda Malalasekara, Managing Director of Sansu International Pvt Ltd, is a seasoned professional with a distinguished academic background and over 15 years of managerial experience in multinational companies. He holds a Ph.D. in Management, an MBA, a Bachelor of Business Administration, and a BSc in Psychology.

Under his leadership, Sansu International has grown into a diversified organization with three key business units: Illumea Skincare, Sansu International Campus, and Success Club Training and Development. Committed to delivering quality products and services at affordable prices, Dr. Malalasekara emphasizes innovation, education, and empowerment in all facets of the business.

His academic and professional expertise drive his vision to create impactful solutions, making Sansu International a trusted name in consultancy, skincare, and education.

  • Master of Business Administration (MBA): His MBA provides a solid foundation in business strategy, management principles, and leadership.
  • Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA): An early academic focus on business principles supports his comprehensive understanding of business operations.
  • B.Sc. in Psychology: His background in psychology enhances his ability to understand and influence human behavior, motivation, and organizational psychology.

Professional Credentials:

  • Certified Professional Marketer: Dr. Malalasekara is certified by the Asia Marketing Federation, highlighting his advanced expertise in marketing strategies and practices.
  • Post Graduate Diploma in Marketing: Awarded by the Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing (SLIM), this diploma underscores his proficiency in marketing and business development.
  • Diplomas in Psychology, Counseling, and Counseling Therapy: His extensive training in these areas contributes to his effective approach to employee motivation and organizational development.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide Illumea in delivering safe, effective, and affordable skincare.

Quality

Commitment to maintaining the highest standards in every product we create. We ensure that every Illumea product meets strict GMP-certified manufacturing standards, guaranteeing consistency, purity, and excellence.

Customer-Centricity

Placing our customers’ safety, satisfaction, and needs at the heart of everything we do. Your well-being drives our work — from ingredient selection to packaging — every decision is made with you in mind.

Integrity

Ensuring transparency and honesty in our processes, ingredients, and claims. We promise clarity in what we offer — no hidden chemicals, no exaggerated promises, just honest skincare you can trust.

Sustainability

Embracing environmentally friendly practices and ethical sourcing of ingredients. Our formulations and packaging are designed to respect nature, reduce waste, and protect the planet for future generations.

Affordability

Striving to make premium skincare accessible to everyone. Luxury should not be out of reach — we deliver world-class skincare at prices that suit every lifestyle.

Innovation

Continuously enhancing our products through research and innovation, blending nature and science. Illumea combines time-tested herbal traditions with modern skincare science, creating products that are both effective and safe.

Wellness & Safety

Guaranteeing that every product is free from harmful chemicals, prioritizing the health and well-being of our customers. We ensure all our products are paraben-free, sulfate-free, and bleaching-free — protecting your skin while enhancing its natural beauty.
